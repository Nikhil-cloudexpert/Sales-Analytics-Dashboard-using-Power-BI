# Project Notes

## Dashboard Objective
The goal of this dashboard is to analyze overall sales performance and identify trends across time, regions, and product categories using Power BI.  
It focuses on providing a clear business overview through key metrics and interactive visuals.

## Key Metrics Used
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Quantity
- Total Orders

These KPIs give a quick summary of business performance at a glance.

## Visual Design Decisions
- KPI cards are placed at the top to highlight overall performance.
- A line chart is used to show monthly sales trends over time.
- Bar and donut charts are used for region-wise and category-wise comparisons.
- A simple and consistent color theme is used to maintain readability.

## Interactivity
- Slicers are used to filter data by time (Year) and other dimensions.
- The dashboard updates dynamically based on user selections, allowing focused analysis.

## Data Preparation
- The dataset was cleaned and transformed using Power Query.
- Column names were standardized and data types were corrected.
- The cleaned data was then loaded into Power BI for analysis.

- Improved understanding of KPI selection and business-focused visualization.
- Learned how to structure and document a Power BI project for GitHub.
